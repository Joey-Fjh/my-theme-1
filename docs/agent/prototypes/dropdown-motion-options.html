Dropdown / Disclosure Motion Options

Standalone prototype for curtain-like open/close directions. Panels are absolutely positioned to avoid layout shift. Click triggers or use Enter/Space. Escape closes the open panel. Tab through menu items when open.

Snaps panels open/closed instantly. Also respects prefers-reduced-motion unless unchecked.

1. Soft curtain

Opacity + clip-path inset from top + slight translateY. Fabric-like drop.

  • New arrivals
  • Best sellers
  • Collections

Best for: header nav, mega menu flyouts.
Risk: low — clip-path widely supported; slightly slower feel at 260ms.

2. Fold down

ScaleY from top + opacity. Restrained hinge, not rubbery.

  • Skincare
  • Body care
  • Sets

Best for: sort/filter dropdowns, collection sub-nav.
Risk: low — can feel slightly "card flip" if scale goes below 0.9.

3. Float sheet

Opacity + translateY only. Clean SaaS-style dropdown.

  • Orders
  • Addresses
  • Sign out

Best for: account menu, utility dropdowns, admin-like UI.
Risk: low — safest default; least distinctive.

4. Gentle reveal with blur

Opacity + small Y + subtle blur settling to clear (3px max).

  • Featured
  • Price: low to high
  • Price: high to low

Best for: premium storefront moments, editorial nav.
Risk: medium — filter repaint cost on low-end mobile; keep blur at 2-3px.

5. Layered curtain

Shell scales open first; content fades/slides in 60ms later.

Our story Ingredients Journal

Best for: header nav with richer panels, mega menu lite.
Risk: low-medium — two-layer DOM; total time ~260ms, watch perceived lag.

6. Compact snap-soft

160ms, 3px travel, slight scale. For tight selectors.

  • English
  • Francais
  • USD ($)
  • EUR (€)

Best for: language selector, currency selector, compact utility menus.
Risk: low — fast enough for frequent toggles; less "premium" feel.

Prototype notes

  • All panels use position: absolute — no surrounding layout shift.
  • Durations: 160-280ms. Easing: soft cubic-bezier curves.
  • Open state: aria-expanded, focus-visible ring, accent border on trigger.
  • Only one dropdown open at a time (click outside or Escape to close).
  • Not wired to theme runtime — visual review only before primitive extraction.
