## Batch B3: super menu tab state, purchase row, gallery dots

**Status:** closed 2026-10-22. Ask review round 4 PASS; the user looked ("没啥问题") and asked to commit. The cascade-pattern audit moved to `backlog.md`.

**Outcome.** (1) In the header super menu's collection tabs, the selected tab looks selected (opacity 1 and underline) and hover and focus brighten a tab. (2) In `.product-info-blocks` (product page, featured product, quick view) at `pc` widths, a purchase button's hover breath (`btn-soft-reverse-breath`: scale up to 1.02, lift up to 2.5px) and its keyboard focus ring are no longer cut at the column edge. Reported by the user, 2026-10-22.

**Cause.** (1) `snippets/header-dropdown-super-menu.liquid` `{% stylesheet %}` sets `.header-dropdown-super-menu__tab { opacity: 0.6 }`; stylesheet CSS is unlayered, so it beats the layered `tab-nav-item--underline-opacity` state rules (`.is-active`, `:hover`, `:focus-visible` opacity 1) in `tailwind/tailwind.components.css` regardless of specificity. (2) `.product-info-blocks` has `pc:overflow-hidden` (carried unchanged from before 5-C2, no recorded reason) and no inline padding: measured at 1440 on the home featured product, the add-to-cart right edge and the clip edge are both at 1401px.

**Implementation surface.** `snippets/header-dropdown-super-menu.liquid` (`{% stylesheet %}`: drop the `opacity` line; inactive tabs go from 0.6 to the component's 0.5). `tailwind/tailwind.components.css` (`.product-info-blocks` at `pc`: `padding-inline: var(--spacing-step-xs)`, about 8px at 1440, reserving room inside the clip; the column's content narrows by that amount on each side); `assets/tailwind.output.css` rebuilt. `sections/featured-product.liquid` (`{% stylesheet %}`: `.featured-product__info-inset` `padding-inline` 0 → `var(--spacing-step-xs)`, round 3). Round 4 (user, 2026-10-22): `snippets/buy-buttons.liquid` `{% stylesheet %}` (`.buy-buttons__quantity > *` `height: auto`); `tailwind/tailwind.components.css` (vertical gallery pagination `bottom: auto`); `snippets/product-gallery-carousel.liquid` (`desktop:mt-0` on the vertical pagination). Round 5: `tailwind/tailwind.input.css` (new `--spacing-button-edge`), used by `.product-info-blocks` at pc, `.featured-product__info-inset`, and `.product-quick-view__info`.

**Review tier: Ask** (raised in round 4: `snippets/product-gallery-carousel.liquid` markup). Follow-up after this batch: audit for the same two patterns elsewhere (unlayered stylesheet properties overriding shared component state; overflow-hidden containers clipping scaled buttons or focus rings).
